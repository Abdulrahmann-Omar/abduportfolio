Preprint · under review · first author

Explaining the Unexplainable? A Ground-Truth Benchmark of Classical, Concept-Based, and Mechanistic XAI on Financial Forecasters

Abdulrahman Omar, Mayada Hadhoud · 2026

A synthetic ground-truth faithfulness benchmark that tests whether XAI attributions recover known feature importance: 23 explainability methods (SHAP, LIME, Integrated Gradients, TCAV, sparse autoencoders, circuit analysis, and more) evaluated across 16 ML/DL models on a leakage-free, near-zero-signal financial forecaster. Popular attribution methods diverge sharply under feature collinearity; results are reported with stock-clustered bootstrap inference and explicit minimum-detectable-effect analysis.

Code & paper Reproducible on Modal · 23 GB news corpus via Apache Spark
